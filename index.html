Tap a photo to compare with the original negative. Auto finds the film frame and crops the holder away; Crop lets you drag the frame yourself (drag corners to resize, inside to move). ⟳ rotates, ⇆ mirrors. Base: tap it, then tap a blank orange film edge — pins the color correction (tap again for auto). Heal: tap it, then tap dust spots on the photo; ↩ undoes the last spot. Set crop & rotation before healing. The histogram shows shadow/highlight clipping percentages — they turn red above 1%. EV adjusts exposure for that frame only. Photos opens the iOS share sheet — tap “Save Image” there to add it to your photo library. Save downloads a file instead (JPEG, or 16-bit TIFF if “TIFF save” is on — TIFF files are big but edit-friendly).
